The innermost guard that owns a route now judges it alone. Until now every `limixGuard` in a route's chain judged it, so an outer guard under `unmatched: 'deny'` refused the routes of a group or prefixed plugin that carried a guard of its own, whatever that guard's rules said. An outer guard now leaves a route to another guard only when that guard comes right after it in the route's chain and the app it was given declares the same route at the same exact path; anything between them (a cache that answers from a store, a middleware) or a plugin mounted under a prefix it does not know means both guards judge, as before. `checkGuard` reports a route with a hook between the two guards (`a hook stands between this guard and the group's guard, so both judge it`): put the group's guard first in the group and `limixGuard` last among the app's own middlewares. Breaking: an outer rule left for a route a group's or plugin's guard judges is no longer taken, so `checkRoutes` throws and the outer guard answers 503 `policy-mismatch`. Move those rules into the group's rules file; `alxiaOperations(group)` lists the routes to cover there. `limixGraphql` endpoints are unchanged: every guard leaves them to their field rules.
