First published version. `@softistx/limix` holds the rules format and its evaluation (`definePolicy`, `evaluateRest`, `evaluateField`, `explain`), coverage of your operations (`./coverage`), signed remote rules (`./remote`) and loading from disk (`./bun`). `@softistx/limix-janus` checks relations through janus. `@softistx/limix-publish` signs a rules file, serves the JWKS and ships the `limix-publish` command.
