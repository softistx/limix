First published version. `@softistx/limix-graxia` guards the fields of a graxia (graphql-js) schema (`guardSchema`, `limixGraxia`), checks `@needs` against the rules (`checkNeeds`, `canOf`) and lists a schema's operations for coverage (`graphqlOperations`, also alone on `./operations`). `@softistx/limix-alxia` guards the routes of an alxia app (`limixGuard`), lists them for coverage (`alxiaOperations`), checks that the rules fit them (`checkRoutes`, `checkGuard`) and serves a GraphQL endpoint behind the same rules (`limixGraphql` on `./graphql`). `@softistx/limix-codegen` registers the API's operations and the rules file's names in `Register` (the `limix-codegen` command and a graphql-codegen plugin) and writes a starter rules file.
